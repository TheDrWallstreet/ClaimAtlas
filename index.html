What this is

ClaimAtlas is a free reader for 38 CFR Part 4, the VA Schedule for Rating Disabilities. It contains the regulation's own wording for every diagnostic code, organized so you can find the one you need without scrolling a 400-page document.

It was built by a retiring Navy Chief who got tired of reading rating criteria on a phone.

What this is not

This site does not give claim advice. It will never tell you what to claim, what to say, or how to answer an examiner. Those things require someone accredited by VA — and this site is deliberately built so it cannot do them.

  • No recommendations about which conditions to file
  • No generated statements, buddy letters, or nexus language
  • No exam coaching
  • No uploading medical records — there is nowhere to put them
  • No promise of any rating, increase, or outcome

Get free help from someone accredited

VA-accredited representatives at organizations like DAV, VFW, the American Legion, and your county or state veterans office represent you for free and can do everything this site refuses to. Under 38 CFR 14.626, they're the only people legally permitted to prepare and present your claim. Find one at va.gov.

Nobody may charge you a fee to file an initial claim. If someone is asking for a cut of your back pay to file one, walk away.

Where the text comes from

Every record is parsed from the official Electronic Code of Federal Regulations and kept in the government's own wording, with whitespace normalized. Each code carries the CFR section it lives in, the date the source was captured, the date Title 38 was last amended, and a direct link to the eCFR page so you can verify it yourself in one click.

Verify before you rely on it. Regulations change without announcement — in February 2026 a rule amending §4.10 took effect and was rescinded ten days later. This site is a convenience, not a legal edition. The eCFR link on every code is the authority.

Privacy

There are no accounts, no logins, no analytics, no trackers, and no server. Your saved list and calculator entries are stored in your own browser and never transmitted anywhere. Close the tab and nothing follows you.
